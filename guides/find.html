the find-anything-in-a-photo prompt

hey, it's KP. you commented FIND, so here it is. here's the exact prompt for finding something in a photo, how to take the photo so it actually has a chance, and the honest limits.

take the photo right

  • more light. daylight or a phone torch at an angle. shadows hide small things.
  • closer, and more than one. split a big area into 2 to 4 overlapping photos rather than one wide shot.
  • full resolution. send the original, not a screenshot.
  • a reference if you can. a photo of the same item (or one like it) helps the model know what it's looking for.

the prompt

I lost [item, with colour, size and shape, e.g. "a white wireless earbud, about the size of a grape"] somewhere in this photo.
1. Scan the whole image carefully, section by section.
2. Tell me the most likely location using simple directions (e.g. "lower left, just right of the large grey rock").
3. Give a confidence level (low, medium, high) and explain what you're seeing.
4. If you can't find it, say so and tell me how to retake the photo.
Don't guess a location just to give an answer.

follow-up that helps

Crop or zoom into the area you think it's in and describe exactly what you see there. Is the shape and colour consistent with [item]?

that's Off-Script Rich: one photo, one question.

good to know

  • it will sometimes be confidently wrong. image models can "see" things that aren't there. the confidence question is there to catch that.
  • the viral clips are the hits. nobody posts the times it didn't work. treat it as a second pair of eyes, not a metal detector.
  • for anything valuable or dangerous, look yourself. keys, medication, a ring. use this to know where to start, not to stop looking.
