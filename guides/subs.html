the subscription audit

hey, it's KP. you commented SUBS, so here it is. here's the audit from the reel. you can do it free with a bank statement in about 20 minutes, and an app is optional. plus the rule in california that makes cancelling easier than companies want you to think.

the free version, 20 minutes

  1. download the last 3 months of statements for every card and bank account. some things only bill quarterly.
  2. search for these words: subscription, monthly, renew, membership, premium, plus, pro, recurring.
  3. also look for small round amounts and anything you don't recognise.
  4. fill in the sheet below.

your rights in california

since 1 July 2025, California's updated automatic renewal law requires businesses to let you cancel through the same way you signed up, so if you subscribed online, you should be able to cancel online. it also requires your clear consent to the auto-renewal and an annual reminder for ongoing subscriptions. if a company makes you call a retention line for something you bought online, that's worth pushing back on.

if you use an app

apps like Rocket Money pull your recurring charges into one list. the fine print matters:

  • premium is pay-what-you-choose, generally $6 to $12 a month according to their help centre.
  • bill negotiation takes 35% to 60% of your first year's savings as a one-time fee, only if they succeed. you pick the percentage.

so if they cut a bill by $20 a month, that's $240 a year saved, and at 35% you'd pay $84 up front. you can often get the same result by calling yourself and asking for the current promo rate.

that's Off-Script Rich: know where every dollar goes before you let anything take a cut.

the prompt

Here are 3 months of my bank and card transactions: [paste or upload CSV].
1. List every charge that repeats monthly, quarterly or yearly, with the amount and merchant.
2. Flag any charge that looks like a free trial that converted to paid.
3. Give me the total per month and per year.
Don't guess merchants you can't identify. List them separately as "unknown".

good to know

  • linking your bank is a trade. any budgeting app sees your transactions. read what it does with the data before you connect.
  • cancelling isn't always deleting. some services keep billing if you only delete the app. cancel in the account settings and screenshot the confirmation.
  • annual plans hide. they show up once a year, so the 3-month check misses them. search your email for "renewal".

proof

  • Barnes & Thornburg: California expands automatic renewal law, new requirements now in effect · AB 2863, effective 1 July 2025, cancel through the same channel used to sign up
  • JD Supra: California's latest automatic renewal law amendments · express consent, annual reminders, click to cancel
