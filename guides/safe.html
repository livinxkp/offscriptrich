where i keep my emergency fund, and why

hey, it's KP. you commented SAFE, so here it is. here's the guide from the reel: how deposit insurance actually works, how a cash account can get coverage past $250,000, and the boring checks i run before i park my emergency fund anywhere. not sponsored, it's just where mine lives.

the boring thing i check: fdic insurance

FDIC insurance covers $250,000 per depositor, per FDIC-insured bank, for each account ownership category. if the bank fails, that's what's protected. most people never get near that limit with an emergency fund, but it's still the first thing to check: is the money actually at an FDIC-insured bank?

how a cash account gets to $8 million

some cash accounts aren't banks themselves. they sweep your money into several partner banks, called program banks. each bank has its own $250,000 limit, so spreading cash across many of them adds up.

Wealthfront's cash account says it sweeps deposits to up to 32 program banks for up to $8 million of FDIC insurance eligibility for an individual account, $16 million for joint. it says it has never charged account fees on the cash account. and it states plainly that Wealthfront isn't a bank.

the checks before you move anything

  1. is it a bank, or does it use program banks? either can work. just know which one it is.
  2. check the bank on FDIC BankFind. it takes a minute.
  3. already bank at a program bank? money you hold there directly and money swept there count together toward that bank's $250,000.
  4. check today's rate. savings yields are variable and change with interest rates. look at the rate and the date on the provider's own page, never a screenshot from a reel.
  5. check how fast you can get it out. an emergency fund you can't reach for a week isn't much of one.

the prompt

I'm comparing places to keep my emergency fund: [list accounts].
For each, tell me:
1. Is it a bank, or does it sweep to program banks? Where does it say so?
2. The FDIC coverage per individual account, and how it's achieved.
3. Any account fees or minimums.
4. How long a withdrawal to my checking account takes.
Link to the provider's own page for each answer. Don't quote an interest rate. Tell me to check it on the day.

good to know

  • "up to" means up to. the $8 million depends on how many program banks have room for your money. read the provider's disclosures.
  • FDIC covers bank failure, not everything. it doesn't cover investments, and the provider itself isn't the insured party when it isn't a bank.
  • how much should be in there? comment PREP on that reel for the calculator with la rent.
