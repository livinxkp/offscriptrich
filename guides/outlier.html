the outlier finder

hey, it's KP. you commented OUTLIER, so here it is. here's the method i used to pick this week's 21 reel ideas, with a worksheet and the prompt that does the maths for you.

viral isn't the signal. outlier is.

a reel with a million views on a page that always gets a million tells you about the page. a reel with a million views on a page that normally gets three thousand tells you about the idea. that's the one worth borrowing.

outlier score = the reel's views ÷ the creator's median views. anything over 10x is an outlier.

the steps

  1. search your niche on instagram or tiktok with 3 or 4 keywords your audience would type.
  2. only use the last 30 days. older hits tell you what worked, not what's working.
  3. for each reel that looks big, open the creator's page and note the views on their last 17 to 20 reels. skip pinned posts, because they're pinned for a reason and skew the number.
  4. take the median, not the average. one monster hit drags an average up.
  5. divide. 10x or more goes on the list.

this week's biggest was a cash-or-finance reel at about 2,004x its creator's median. the rest of the list ran from 12x up.

keep: 10x and up, posted in the last 30 days

the prompt

Here are reels from my niche with their view counts, and for each creator, the view counts of their last 17-20 non-pinned reels:
[paste]
1. Calculate each creator's median views.
2. Calculate each reel's outlier score (views / median).
3. List every reel at 10x or above, sorted by score.
4. For each, describe the topic and format in one line, and what made it work (hook, format, timing).
5. Flag any format that appears in 3 or more outliers from different creators.
Show the median and score for each so I can check the maths.

borrow the topic, own the angle

once you have the list, keep the topic and the format, then make it yours. your city, your numbers, your story. that's Off-Script Rich.

good to know

  • one outlier can be luck. a format showing up in 3 or more outliers from different creators in a few weeks is a trend. one is a hint.
  • check the topic still has time. news-driven outliers (an election, a crash) expire fast.
  • don't copy the video. copying someone's script and edit word for word isn't the move. the idea is fair game, the execution should be yours.
  • don't scrape with tools that log into your account. look it up by hand or use tools that don't need your login.
