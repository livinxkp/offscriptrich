pricing ad rights and spark codes

hey, it's KP. you commented RIGHTS, so here it is. here's the one-page guide from the reel: what ad authorization actually hands a brand, how i price it separately from the video, and the exact reply i send.

the line to look for

in the brand email from the reel the line was "ad authorization must be enabled when posting". other versions say "spark code", "whitelisting", "allowlisting", "usage rights" or "partnership ad". they all mean the same thing: the brand wants to run your video as a paid ad.

what a spark code is

on TikTok, you turn on ad authorization in a post's ad settings and generate a code for that one video. you pick how long it lasts: 7, 30, 60 or 365 days. the brand pastes the code into TikTok Ads Manager and can then put money behind your post and show it to people who've never heard of you. instagram's version is called partnership ads.

a code covers one post. if they want three videos, that's three codes.

why it's a separate price

  • commission pays for sales you make. people buy through your link because they trust you.
  • ad rights pay for reach they buy. their budget, their targeting, your face. on a commission-only deal, you can end up starring in their ad and get paid only when your own link converts.

two different things, so two different prices. that's Off-Script Rich.

how i price it

THE VIDEO (concept, script, shoot, edit)      $______   mine on the reel: $1,100
AD AUTHORIZATION / USAGE                      $______   mine on the reel: $500 for 14 days
  longer window = higher fee
  more than one platform = higher fee
  whitelisting from my handle vs. their handle: say which
COMMISSION (on top, not instead)              ___%      mine on the reel: 15%
EXCLUSIVITY (if they ask for it)              $______   price per month, per category

pick a code length that covers the window you agreed. TikTok's options are 7, 30, 60 or 365 days, so a 14-day deal means a 30-day code with the window written into the agreement. you can turn authorization off in the app when the paid window ends.

the reply

Hi [name], thanks for reaching out, I'd love to work with [brand].

Quick question before we go further: is there a paid fee for the video, or is this commission only?

For reference, here's how I'd price it:
- dedicated video: $[video]
- ad authorization for [x] days: $[usage]
- [x]% commission on top

Happy to adjust the usage window if your budget sits somewhere else.
Could you also confirm when payment is released after the video goes live?

[your name]

good to know

  • get the terms in writing before you generate a code. once the code is shared, they can run the ad for as long as the code lasts.
  • disclose it. paid or gifted, the FTC wants the relationship disclosed in the video itself, not just the caption. the platform's paid-partnership label alone isn't enough.
  • these are my numbers, not a benchmark. for how i get to the base rate, comment RATES on that reel.
