chatgpt photo codes, the starter pack

hey, it's KP. you commented CODES, so here it is. here's the la magazine prompt from the reel plus five more i use. upload your own photos, paste one code and you get a styled set back in a couple of minutes.

how to use these

  1. open chatgpt and upload 1 to 4 clear photos of yourself. good light, face visible, no sunglasses.
  2. paste one code. change only what's in [square brackets].
  3. if your face drifts, reply: "keep my face exactly as in the uploaded photos, change nothing about my features."

1. the la magazine spread

Turn these 4 photos into a 4-page LA editorial magazine spread. Golden hour light, film grain, bold serif headlines, my face unchanged. Page 1 is a full-bleed cover with a one-word masthead. Pages 2 to 4 mix one large photo with short pull-quote captions. Magazine name: [NAME].

2. the film strip

Make a vertical photo-booth film strip with 4 frames using my uploaded photos. Black-and-white 35mm film look, slight light leaks, natural expressions, my face unchanged. Handwritten date at the bottom: [DATE].

3. golden hour editorial

Restyle my uploaded photo as a high-end editorial portrait shot at golden hour on a rooftop in Los Angeles. 85mm lens look, shallow depth of field, warm skin tones, subtle film grain, my face and outfit unchanged. Vertical 4:5.

4. the campaign poster

Turn my uploaded photo into a minimalist campaign poster. Solid [COLOR] background, me cut out cleanly, one huge lowercase word across the top: [WORD]. Small text at the bottom: [LINE]. Clean, modern, lots of empty space. Vertical 4:5.

5. the cafe candid

Place me from the uploaded photos into a candid photo at a sunny sidewalk cafe in [CITY]. Shot from across the street like a street-style photographer, natural pose, coffee on the table, soft morning light, my face unchanged. Looks like a real photo, not an illustration.

6. the product flat lay

Create an overhead flat-lay photo of [PRODUCT] on [SURFACE, e.g. white linen]. Soft daylight from the left, a few small props that fit the brand, lots of negative space for text at the top. Looks like a real product photo for an Instagram ad. Square 1:1.

good to know

  • faces drift. image models sometimes change features slightly. regenerate or use the fix-line above, and don't post anything that doesn't look like you.
  • only upload photos you own. don't use other people's photos or faces without their permission.
  • text in images can come out wrong. check spelling on posters and mastheads before you post.
  • results vary by plan and model. image features and limits change. if a code fails, shorten it to the first sentence and try again.
