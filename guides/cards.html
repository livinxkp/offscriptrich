the best card for each category

hey, it's KP. you commented CARDS, so here it is. the reel gave you one card per category. this is the one-page breakdown: what each card actually earns right now, what it costs, the catch on each, and a calculator that runs your own monthly spending through all four so you're not choosing off someone else's lifestyle.

who this is for

you, if you pay your card off in full every month and you're putting real spending on a card that earns 1% or nothing. skip this entirely if you carry a balance. card interest runs far above any reward on this page, so paying that down beats every card here.

the four, with the real numbers

1. dining and groceries · American Express Gold

4x points at restaurants (on up to $50,000 a year) and 4x at US supermarkets (on up to $25,000 a year), 3x on flights booked direct or through Amex Travel, 1x on everything else. annual fee $325. it comes with monthly credits (dining partners, Uber Cash, Dunkin', Resy) that can offset a lot of the fee, but only if you'd have spent at those places anyway. points, not cash: their value depends on how you redeem them.

2. travel · Capital One Venture X

10x miles on hotels and rental cars and 5x on flights, when booked through Capital One Travel. 2x on everything else. annual fee $395, with up to $300 a year in travel credit (also only through Capital One Travel) and 10,000 bonus miles every anniversary. if you'll use the credit, the real fee is much smaller. if you won't book through their portal, the 10x and 5x don't apply.

3. gas and ev charging · Costco Anywhere Visa by Citi

5% on gas at Costco, 4% on other gas and EV charging (together on up to $7,000 a year, then 1%), 3% on restaurants and eligible travel, 2% at Costco, 1% everywhere else. no annual fee, but you need a Costco membership. the catch: rewards arrive once a year as a certificate with your February statement.

4. starting out · Discover it Cash Back

5% back on rotating categories you activate each quarter, on up to $1,500 of spend per quarter, then 1%. 1% on everything else. no annual fee. for October to December 2026 the 5% categories are entertainment, restaurants and utilities. Discover cards are moving under Capital One after the merger, with reward structures staying the same for now. the reel said it's the easiest approval with no history. i couldn't verify that, so check the pre-approval tool on the issuer's site, which doesn't hurt your score.

the one rule

pay the full statement balance, every month, automatically. one month of interest wipes out a year of 4% back. set autopay to "statement balance", not "minimum", the day you get the card. everything else on this page only works if that's true. that's Off-Script Rich.

good to know

  • terms change. earn rates, credits and fees above were checked on 12 October 2026. issuers change them, and the issuer's own terms page is the final word before you apply.
  • points aren't cash. Amex points and Capital One miles can be worth more than 1 cent through transfer partners, or less if you redeem for statement credit. the calculator uses 1 cent so it doesn't flatter the fee cards.
  • every application is a hard inquiry. it dips your score a little for a while. don't open four cards because of one reel. pick the one that fits your biggest category.
  • no affiliate links. i don't earn anything if you open any of these. there are no referral links on this page.
