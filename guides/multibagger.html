the nvidia paper trail

hey, it's KP. you commented MULTIBAGGER, so here it is. the reel named two companies Nvidia put money into this year. this is the full breakdown: every disclosed Nvidia stake and investment deal i could verify, the filing each one lives in, and how to catch the next one yourself instead of waiting for a reel.

who this is for

you, if you invest in AI and want to know where the biggest company in the space is putting its own money. skip it if you want someone to tell you what to buy. this is a map of disclosures, not a list of picks, and Nvidia owning something has never guaranteed it goes up.

the two from the reel

Marvell Technology (MRVL). on 31 March 2026, Nvidia announced a $2 billion investment in Marvell alongside a partnership that opens Nvidia's NVLink Fusion system to Marvell's custom AI chips and networking. Marvell builds custom silicon and the networking chips that connect data centres together. on 2 June, at Computex, Jensen Huang called Marvell the next trillion-dollar company, and its shares jumped more than 25% in premarket trading.

IREN (IREN). the reel said "iron", the ticker is IREN. it builds and runs data centres for AI training and cloud. in May 2026, Bloomberg reported Nvidia would invest up to $2.1 billion in the company, alongside a large AI cloud contract and an infrastructure partnership. "up to" matters: check IREN's own filings for what has actually been funded.

what nvidia's own filing shows

every quarter, any investment manager holding over $100 million in US-listed stocks has to file a Form 13F listing them, within 45 days of the quarter ending. Nvidia files one. it's the cleanest public list of what it holds.

its 13F for the quarter ending 31 March 2026 (filed 15 May) listed seven holdings:

the next one, for the quarter ending 30 June 2026, was filed on 14 August with eight holdings worth about $63.4 billion, led by Intel, SpaceX, CoreWeave and Coherent. open the filing itself on EDGAR for the full list and share counts. third-party sites summarise it, the SEC copy is the source.

notice what's missing: a $2 billion deal announced on the last day of a quarter may not show up in that quarter's 13F, and stakes in private companies never do. that's why you need the other two filings below.

how to catch the next one

three filings, three speeds.

FASTEST   8-K / press release from the company RECEIVING the money
          -> filed within days. search EDGAR full-text for
             "NVIDIA" + "investment agreement" or "securities purchase"

MEDIUM    Schedule 13D or 13G
          -> filed when anyone crosses 5% of a public company.
             shows up on the target company's EDGAR page

SLOWEST   Nvidia's own 13F
          -> quarterly, up to 45 days after quarter end.
             complete for US-listed holdings, but late

the set-up that takes five minutes: on SEC EDGAR full-text search, search "NVIDIA Corporation" "purchase agreement", filter to 8-K, and save the search. check it on Mondays. you'll see the next deal in the filing, the same week the headlines do, without anyone's spin on it.

Here is an 8-K / press release: [paste or attach].
1. how much is Nvidia investing, and is it committed or "up to"?
2. what does Nvidia get: shares, warrants, a contract, all three?
3. at what price per share, and how does that compare to the
   price the day before the announcement?
4. is there a commercial deal attached where the company
   commits to buy Nvidia products?
Quote the filing for each answer. Say "not stated" if it isn't.

question 4 is the one people skip. a lot of these deals send money in and send orders back out to Nvidia. that's not bad, it's just worth knowing what you're looking at. that's Off-Script Rich.

good to know

  • a stake is not an endorsement. Nvidia invests in customers and partners for strategic reasons: to secure supply, grow demand for its chips, or keep a seat at the table. that's a different goal from yours.
  • the price has often already moved. Marvell's 25% premarket jump happened the morning after Huang spoke. by the time it's a reel, the news is usually in the price.
  • 13Fs are a snapshot. they show holdings on the last day of the quarter, filed weeks later. Nvidia may have bought or sold since. they also leave out private companies and most non-US listings.
