the best-price calendar

hey, it's KP. you commented MONTH, so here it is. here's the full year: the eight things from the reel with the month they usually hit their best price, the rest of the calendar, and the two checks i run so a "sale" is actually a sale.

whatwhenwhy
a new carlast week of decemberdealers chase monthly, quarterly and yearly targets at the same time. Edmunds data has December as the best month for discounts off MSRP.

two checks before you buy

  1. check the price history. a price tracker like camelcamelcamel for Amazon shows whether the "sale" price is actually low or just back to normal.
  2. check it's the same model. some black friday tvs are special models made for the sale. compare the full model number, not the screen size.

that's Off-Script Rich: same money, just better timing.

the prompt

I want to buy [item] in the next [x] months, budget $[amount].
1. When does this kind of item usually hit its best price in the US, and why?
2. What should I compare (model numbers, specs) to make sure a sale model isn't a lower-spec version?
3. Is there a reason not to wait (price trend, a new model coming)?
Be honest if timing doesn't matter much for this item.

good to know

  • patterns, not promises. these are retail habits, not rules. supply problems or a hot product can break them.
  • don't buy things because they're on sale. the calendar is for things you were already going to buy.
  • the car one needs negotiating. december gives you leverage. it doesn't apply the discount for you.

proof

  • AARP: the best time of the year to buy a car · cites Edmunds data on December year-end savings
  • Bankrate: the best time of year to buy everything · seasonal sale patterns by category
  • AAA: best time to buy calendar · mattresses on memorial day, patio furniture as summer ends
  • camelcamelcamel · free Amazon price history
