the three-question brand email check

hey, it's KP. you commented BLAST, so here it is. here's the checklist from the reel. three questions, about a minute per email, and the reply i send instead of filling out yet another form.

question one: is there a number?

"guaranteed payment", "competitive rates" and "paid collaboration" are not numbers. a real offer either names a fee or asks for yours.

no number anywhere: not a red flag on its own, but it means you set the price, not them.

question two: who decides if i get paid?

look for lines like "only videos that follow the official guidelines are eligible for payment" or "payment upon approval". if the brand decides after you've filmed whether your work counts, you're doing the work on spec.

what you want instead: a locked brief before you film, a set number of revisions, and a payment date tied to the video going live.

question three: is it to me, or to everyone?

signs it's a mass blast:

  • the same subject line more than once in a week.
  • "spots are limited" or a countdown.
  • a form or application link instead of a reply.
  • nothing in the email that shows they've watched a single video of yours.

a mass email isn't automatically a scam. it's a numbers game, and you're one of a few thousand names.

the scorecard

BRAND EMAIL CHECK                    from: ______   date: ______
1. is there a number?                         Y / N
2. do i know payment terms BEFORE filming?    Y / N
3. is it written to me specifically?          Y / N

3 yes  -> reply properly, this is a real lead
1-2    -> send the rate reply below, let them qualify themselves
0      -> archive. if they send it again, archive again.

that's Off-Script Rich: let the email prove itself before you give it your time.

the reply instead of the form

Hi, thanks for thinking of me.
Rather than filling out the form, here's my rate for a dedicated video: $[rate], including [x] round of revisions once the brief is locked.
Ad usage is priced separately. Happy to share my media kit if this fits your budget.
Could you confirm the payment amount and when it's released after the video goes live?

good to know

  • never pay to join a "paid" campaign. a brand paying you doesn't need your card number.
  • don't send ID or bank details until there's a signed agreement with a real company name on it.
  • a good deal still needs scoring. once one passes, comment DEAL on that reel for the 100-point scorecard.
