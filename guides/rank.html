the first $1,000 prompts, and my ranked list

hey, it's KP. you commented RANK, so here it is. here are the three prompts and the bonus from the reel, written out so you can paste them, plus the ranked list i promised: the routes to a first paying customer, ordered by how fast someone with no audience actually gets paid.

who this is for

you, if you want your first $1,000 from something other than a job and you don't know where to start. skip it if you're looking for passive income. every route below is you doing work for someone who pays you. that's why it's fast.

the three prompts

run them in order, in the same chat. each one builds on the last. fill in the brackets honestly. the output is only as good as what you admit about your skills and time.

PROMPT ONE
Give me two ways to make my first $1,000 in the next 30 days.
Constraints:
- skills I already have: [list everything, even boring ones]
- hours per week I can give this: [number]
- money I can spend: [amount]
- no audience, no followers, no existing customers
For each way, tell me who pays, what they pay for, a realistic
price, and how many customers I'd need to reach $1,000.
PROMPT TWO
For the better of those two, who is the one real, named person I
should learn from? Someone who has actually done this and teaches
it publicly. Tell me what to read or watch from them first, and
what to ignore.
PROMPT THREE
Now answer as that person would. Build me a 20-day plan to my
first paying customer, spending under $1,000. Each day gets one
task that takes under two hours. Day 1 starts with what I already
have. By day 10 I should have asked at least five real people to
pay me.
BONUS
Ask me three yes-or-no questions that would reveal what's most
likely to stop me from finishing this plan. After I answer, change
the plan to account for my answers.

my ranked list

this is my opinion, not a study. ranked by how fast someone with no audience usually gets the first payment, fastest first.

  1. sell a skill you already have to someone you already know. editing, design, bookkeeping, tutoring, admin. one message to ten people in your contacts. the warm list is the fastest money there is.
  2. a local, physical service. moving help, cleaning, assembly, errands. boring, in demand, paid same day.
  3. flip things. buy underpriced, sell properly listed. good AI-written listings and better photos are an unfair advantage here.
  4. an AI-assisted service for small local businesses. product descriptions, menus, a simple site, a month of social captions. you're selling the result, not "AI".
  5. UGC for small brands. short videos of you using a product, paid per video. needs a small portfolio first, which you can make with things you own.
  6. freelance platforms. real money, slower start. the first review takes the longest.
  7. a digital product. templates, guides, presets. great margins, slowest first dollar without an audience. this is where you go second, not first.

the pattern: the closer you are to a person who already trusts you, the faster you get paid. audience-dependent routes are at the bottom on purpose.

then do the next action before you close the tab. that's Off-Script Rich.

good to know

  • no ai can promise you money. these prompts make a plan. the plan only pays when you message real people and ask them to pay you. that step is where most people stop.
  • check anything with legal weight. local services can need a business licence, and some trades need insurance. ask Claude what applies in your city, then confirm on your city or county website.
  • the 20-day plan will be too optimistic. it always is. if day 10 arrives and nobody has paid, go back to the bonus prompt and tell it what actually happened.
