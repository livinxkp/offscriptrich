five claude updates, one page

hey, it's KP. you commented CLAUDE, so here it is. here's the one-page breakdown of the five updates from the reel, checked against Anthropic's own announcements, with who actually has access right now and the one thing i'd do with each.

who this is for

you, if you use Claude for work and lost track somewhere in September. skip it if you're on the free plan and don't plan to upgrade. most of what's below is on paid plans first.

1. opus 5.5

the new flagship, released 22 September 2026.

Anthropic says it costs 40% less to run than Opus 5 on typical workloads and writes output more than 30% faster. on the API the list price dropped from $5 to $4 per million input tokens and from $25 to $20 per million output tokens, and the 40% figure comes from it also being more efficient with tokens. Anthropic says it performs at the level of Claude Fable 5.1 on most work. five-hour usage limits went up on Pro, Max, Team and seat-based Enterprise.

do this: the long multi-step job you gave up on because it ran out of steam halfway (a big spreadsheet clean-up, a full site rebuild, a research report), run it again.

2. chat and cowork are one thing now

announced 16 September 2026.

you no longer pick between chat and Cowork. chat, Cowork and Artifacts sit in one window and Claude routes the request to the right mode itself. it came with dedicated Docs and Presentations you can share by link, edit on your phone, and download as PDF or PowerPoint. the caveat: it rolled out to Pro and Max first, with Free and Team later and no date given.

do this: stop opening a new tab for every task type. ask for the finished thing ("make the deck", "write the doc") and let it pick the mode.

3. claude for small business got 43 workflows

announced 15 September 2026.

43 ready-made workflows and 27 new integrations, including Shopify, Stripe, Square, Xero, Gusto, Salesforce, TikTok, Zoom and Zapier, on top of the original QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot, Canva and Google Workspace connections. it runs in the Cowork side of the desktop app and is on every paid plan. Anthropic says it had been installed over 900,000 times since May.

do this: connect the one tool you open most (your store, your payments, your books) and ask the boring question you never have time for. "what did i actually make last month after fees" is a good first one.

4. projects can split one goal across threads

announced 17 September 2026, in beta.

you give a project one goal. a coordinator thread scopes it and starts worker threads for the parts, and the project's memory carries across all of them. right now this is a Claude Code feature: workers run as cloud coding sessions and hand back changes as pull requests. the beta started with Pro and Max users running cloud sessions, with chat, Cowork, Team and Enterprise promised "in the following weeks".

do this: if you code, pick a chore that touches many files (rename something everywhere, update a dependency) and let the coordinator split it. if you don't, file this under "coming to you soon" and move on.

5. salesforce is inside claude

announced 15 September 2026, in beta on all paid plans.

a plugin built with Salesforce that brings your accounts, opportunities and pipeline into Claude under your existing Salesforce permissions. it has 37 skills, including a daily brief, call prep that pulls from Salesforce, Slack and email, deal reviews, and post-meeting follow-ups written from your call notes. by default it asks before it writes anything back to Salesforce. an admin has to switch it on once for the organisation.

do this: if you sell for a living, use call prep before your next meeting and the follow-up skill straight after it. those two are the hour a day it gives back.

the prompt to try all five at once

paste this into Claude after the updates land on your plan. it turns five announcements into a plan for your week.

Here is what I do for work: [your job in two sentences].
Here are the tools I use daily: [list them].

Claude recently shipped: Opus 5.5, chat and Cowork merged into one
window with Docs and Presentations, Claude for Small Business with
Shopify/Stripe/Square/Xero/Salesforce integrations, a Projects
coordinator that splits one goal across threads, and Salesforce
in Claude.

For each one, tell me in one line whether it's relevant to me.
For the two that are most relevant, give me one specific task I
can hand you this week, written as the exact prompt I'd send.

that's Off-Script Rich: you don't need every update, you need the two that change your week.

good to know

  • rollouts are staged. "announced" isn't "on your account". three of these five started on Pro and Max, or in beta, so if you can't see one yet, that's probably why.
  • the 40% is a workload number. list prices fell 20%. the 40% is Anthropic's figure for typical workloads at default settings, because it also uses fewer tokens. your mileage depends on what you run.
  • connectors read your data. anything you connect (your store, your CRM, your books) becomes something Claude can read. connect what the task needs, not everything you own.
