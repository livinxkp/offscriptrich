step one: record the sample

  1. ElevenLabs recommends 1 to 2 minutes of good audio for an instant clone. more isn't better past that.
  2. record in a quiet room, phone 20cm from your mouth, no music.
  3. talk the way you talk on camera. read a caption or two, then just explain something you know well.
  4. export as MP3 at 192kbps or higher, which is their recommended format.

step two: make the clone

  1. in ElevenLabs, go to voices, click the plus icon, choose instant voice clone.
  2. upload the sample, name it, confirm it's your voice.
  3. test it: paste a caption into text to speech and pick your voice.

step three: dub a reel

  1. open dubbing and upload the finished reel.
  2. pick the target language. their dubbing page lists 90+ languages.
  3. download, watch it all the way through, then post it as its own reel or on a second account.

good to know

  • only clone your own voice. cloning someone else's voice without permission is against ElevenLabs' rules and can be illegal.
  • tell people. if a reel is AI-dubbed, say so on screen or in the caption. it's the honest move and platforms increasingly label it anyway.
  • prices and credits change. check the pricing page before you pay.
  • mistranslations happen. ask a native speaker to watch one before you post a batch.
