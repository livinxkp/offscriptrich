google maps + claude, the walkthrough

hey, it's KP. you commented MAPS, so here it is. here's the full walkthrough from the reel with the exact claude prompt for the one-page site, the message i'd send with the preview, and the part that's actually hard.

who this is for

you, if you want a first paying client without an audience. skip it if you want passive income. this is a service, and the hard part is talking to people.

step one: find them

  1. open google maps and search a service near you. barbers, taco spots, nail salons, mechanics.
  2. open each listing. you're looking for lots of good reviews and either no website link or a site that's clearly old or broken.
  3. keep a list: name, phone, rating, number of reviews, what their site is missing.

step two: build the preview

screenshot their maps listing (name, hours, a few reviews, photos) and give it to claude with this:

Build a one-page website for this local business using only the information in the screenshot I've attached.
Include: business name, a one-line description, hours, address with a "get directions" link, a click-to-call phone button, 3 real reviews quoted exactly from the screenshot with the reviewer's first name, and a simple photo section using placeholders I can swap.
Mobile-first, fast, clean, one HTML file. Colours that fit the type of business.
Do not invent reviews, prices, services or awards. If something isn't in the screenshot, leave a clearly marked placeholder.

step three: put it online

you need a link you can send. if you're using claude, you can publish the page and share the link. or drop the HTML file into a free static host. keep the preview private to that one business until they say yes.

step four: show it first

Hey [name], I'm [your name], I live nearby and love [business].
I noticed you don't have a website, so I made you a quick preview using your Google reviews: [link]
If you like it, I can make it live on your own domain this week for $[price]. If not, no worries at all, it's yours to look at either way.

so what's the hard part? it isn't the building. it's walking in or messaging and showing it to them. send ten. expect most to ignore you. one yes is the whole point.

that's Off-Script Rich: build first, ask second.

good to know

  • don't invent anything on their site. real reviews, real hours, no fake services or prices. the prompt says this for a reason.
  • reach out like a person. one message per business, in person or a direct message. mass cold email has legal rules (the FTC's CAN-SPAM guide below) and it also just works worse.
  • price it simply. one setup fee plus an optional small monthly fee for hosting and edits. agree it in writing before you buy them a domain.
  • check claude's code on your phone before you send it. tap every button.

proof

  • FTC: CAN-SPAM Act compliance guide for business · rules for commercial email
